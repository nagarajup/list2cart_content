Delete your List2Cart account

List2Cart (by AniApps) works without an account. An account is created only if you sign in with Google to share lists with family. You can delete it at any time.

Delete in the app

  1. Open List2Cart and go to Account.
  2. Tap Delete account under your name.
  3. Confirm with Delete.

Can't use the app?

Email nagarajup.andap@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my List2Cart account". We delete it within 7 days.

Delete some data without deleting your account

  • A shared list: open the list in My Lists → Share → Stop sharing. Its online copy and items are deleted right away, and family members lose access.
  • Automatic clean-up: any shared list is deleted automatically 10 days after its last update (an edit or a tick).
  • A list shared with you: open it in the Shared tab → ⋮ → Remove from Shared with me.
  • Lists on your phone: delete them in My Lists, or clear the app's data / uninstall the app. They are never stored on our servers.
  • Items or photos you submitted with "Add your own item": email nagarajup.andap@gmail.com with the item names, and we delete them within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email address and profile photo link from Google.
  • Every list you shared online, with its items. Family members lose access to them.
  • Your membership in lists others shared with you.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Lists saved only on your phone are not affected; remove them in the app or by uninstalling it. We keep no copies after deletion, apart from routine encrypted backups at our hosting provider, which expire within 30 days.
