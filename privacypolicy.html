Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the List2Cart Android app ("List2Cart", "the App"), published by AniApps ("we", "our", "us"), handles information when you use it.

In short
  • You don't need an account. Your shopping lists are stored on your phone.
  • Items you add yourself (and an optional photo) are sent to us to improve the product catalog.
  • We use Google Firebase to understand app usage and fix crashes.
  • No ads, no location tracking, and we never sell your data.

1. Information stored only on your phone

Your shopping lists, quantities, notes and history are saved in the App's private storage on your device. We do not receive them. If you share a list as text or PDF, it goes only to the app or person you choose. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes this information from your phone.

2. Information sent to us

a. Items you add yourself

When an item is not in our catalog and you use "Add your own item", we receive:

  • the item name, quantity and note you entered;
  • the photo, if you choose to add one (taken with the camera or picked from your gallery);
  • the App version and a random ID created when the App is installed (not your name, phone number or device ID).

We use this only to review and improve the List2Cart catalog (for example, adding commonly requested products). It is not used for advertising and is not linked to your identity. We keep it only as long as needed for that review.

b. Product catalog

The App downloads the product catalog from our server. This request does not include personal information, although our hosting provider may process your IP address to deliver it.

c. Usage and crash information

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand which features are used and to fix problems. They collect information such as app interactions, device model, operating system version, app instance identifiers, a device identifier, and crash logs. This information helps us improve the App and is not used for advertising.

3. Permissions

  • Camera – only when you choose to take a photo of an item you are adding.
  • Microphone – only when you tap the microphone for voice search. Speech is converted to text by your phone's speech recognition service (usually Google) and the App only receives the recognized text.
  • Photos – when you choose "Choose from gallery", Android's photo picker gives the App access only to the single photo you select.
  • Internet – to download the catalog and send the information described above.

The App does not access your location, contacts, or files beyond what is described here.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with service providers that run the App for us:

  • Supabase – hosts the product catalog and the items you add (Supabase Privacy Policy).
  • Google Firebase – analytics and crash reporting (Google Privacy Policy).

We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Data security

Information sent from the App is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Items and photos you add can be read only by us, not by other users. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

6. Your choices

  • Adding photos and notes is optional.
  • You can deny or revoke the camera and microphone permissions at any time in your phone's settings; the rest of the App keeps working.
  • To ask about or request deletion of information you submitted, contact us at the email below.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
