FAQ

Questions, answered

Is List2Cart free?

Yes. The app is free to download and use, with no ads. You only sign in with Google if you want to share a list live.

How does live sharing work?

Open a list, tap Share and choose Live link. Send it on WhatsApp. Family members open it in List2Cart and tick items as they buy. Only you can edit the list, and you can stop sharing any time.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The catalog and your lists are stored on your phone. Internet is only needed to share a list or get catalog updates.

Where is my data stored?

Your lists stay on your phone. Only lists you share live are stored online. See our privacy policy for details.

Is it available on iPhone?

List2Cart is available for Android phones on Google Play.
