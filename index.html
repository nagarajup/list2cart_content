How it works

From list to cart in three steps

1

2

Review and add notes

See your whole list at a glance. Add notes like brand, size or "organic" so the right item comes home.

3

Shop and tick off

Share the list on WhatsApp, or take it to the shop. Move items from To Buy to In Cart as you go.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is List2Cart free?

Yes. The app is free to download and use, with no ads and no sign-up.

Which languages are supported?

Item names are available in English, Telugu and Hindi. You can show the local name with English alongside.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The catalog and your lists are stored on your phone. Internet is only needed to share a list or get catalog updates.

Where is my data stored?

Your lists stay on your phone. See our privacy policy for details.

Is it available on iPhone?

List2Cart is available for Android phones on Google Play.
